## Provenance

Collected by the authors from a single production line, 2004 photographs of loose dry leaf,
no people and no location metadata in the files. The photographs are of a commercial
product, which is why the non-commercial clause is there: the images identify a
manufacturer's product line and are not ours to license for resale.
